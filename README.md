# Arnoldo Alonso

I build accessible, auditable software for public-interest research, veteran services, and technical education. I founded Brasidas Strategies, lead the design of Ayala, and served in the U.S. Army during Operation Iraqi Freedom and Operation New Dawn.

## Selected work

- [Founder-Architect portfolio and publications](https://alonsobrx.github.io/founder-architect/)
- [Veterans Benefits Capacity and Due Process Observatory](https://alonsobrx.github.io/founder-architect/observatory/) — reproducible research on VA workload, appeals, and governance.
- [Founder-Architect source code](https://github.com/alonsobrx/founder-architect) — bilingual publications, source data, validation, and corrections.

My engineering practice focuses on accessible web interfaces, low-bandwidth delivery, reproducible analysis, and human review of consequential decisions. My research interests include event-driven and neuromorphic computing.
